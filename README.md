Entry for the [One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off](https://make.fun/competitions/u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73) on make.fun — one original one-line office joke, written to get the biggest laugh from the judge.

## The joke

> Someone labeled my lunch "evidence" in the office fridge, and now HR wants to know what crime I committed.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`. The line was picked from 10 candidates in `candidates.md` through internal pairwise testing against the judge's criteria (one-line office joke, sharp and surprising punchline, instantly clear, fresh, tight wording) — it won 4 pairwise matchups.
